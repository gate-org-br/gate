<g-callout>

The g-callout component highlights content that deserves stronger visual emphasis than normal text. Use it for conceptual notes, section summaries and important explanations that should stand out from the surrounding page. Use semantic content such as headings, paragraphs, lists and dividers inside the callout. For short status messages, warnings and tips, g-message is usually a better fit.
