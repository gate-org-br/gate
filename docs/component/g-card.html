<g-card>

The g-card component groups related content into a compact panel. It is useful for previews, summaries, dashboard widgets and navigable content blocks with a clear internal structure. It supports common sections such as header, section, nav and footer, and may also include an image or icon before the content. Use it for self-contained panels rather than long conceptual text.
